👕 Comprehensive International & Inclusive Sizing Reference

Inclusive cross-border sizing guide covering Petite, Regular, Tall, Plus sizes, and Kids/Youth apparel and footwear.

🌍 Global Sizing Diversity Note: International storefronts vary significantly in cut and proportions. Petite ranges generally accommodate heights under 5'4" (162 cm), while Tall ranges accommodate heights over 5'9" (175 cm). Plus / Extended Sizes scale proportion and rise rather than just width. Always cross-reference merchant chest/waist measurements in centimeters or inches.
