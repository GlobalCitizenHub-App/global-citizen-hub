📐 Estimation Rationale & Methodology

  • Supply Chain Positioning: Estimates factor in local agricultural and municipal price controls versus imported luxury goods markups.
  • Operating Profile Scaling: Costs flex dynamically based on whether you rely on public municipal systems (Economical), hybrid expatriate services (Moderate), or exclusive concierge/michelin-standard infrastructure (Premium).
  • Purchasing Power Parity (PPP): Values are calibrated against metropolitan Consumer Price Indexes (CPI) to ensure alignment with real-world spending norms.
